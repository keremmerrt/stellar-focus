Delete your Stellar Focus account

Stellar Focus by Kerch Labs · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Deleting your account is permanent. It removes your account and the backup stored with it, and we cannot restore them afterwards.

How to request deletion

Option 1: in the app

Open Stellar Focus, go to Settings and tap Request Account Deletion. Your email app opens with a prepared message. Send it.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to stellarfocus.support@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Stellar Focus account deletion request
  • The email address of your account
  • A short note confirming that you want the account deleted
Send deletion request

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you do not hear back, check your spam folder or write to us again.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, username, password and, if you signed in with Google, the name, profile picture link and account ID that Google shared
  • Your cloud backup: focus sessions, galaxy objects and Stardust history
  • The session checks stored for your account

What stays on your phone

Data on your phone is separate from your account. To remove it, use Settings → Reset All Data in the app or uninstall the app. Reset All Data alone clears only your phone and keeps your account and backup.

Data we may keep

We do not keep account data after deletion, unless the law requires it or we need it to handle a security or fraud problem. In that case we keep only what is needed, and only for as long as it is needed.

After deletion

  • You can no longer sign in with that account.
  • To use Stellar Focus again, you need to create a new account.

Contact

Questions? Email stellarfocus.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
