Privacy Policy

Stellar Focus by Kerch Labs · Last updated: November 23, 2026 (version 0.3.0)

Stellar Focus keeps your sessions and your galaxy on your phone. Because the app needs an account, it also keeps a backup of that data in your account, so you can restore it on a new phone. If you add friends, they can see your username, your galaxy and how many minutes you focused this week, and nothing else. Everything else you can share, such as appearing on the global board, starts switched off. We do not sell your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Stellar Focus is an Android app made by Kerem Mert, who publishes it on Google Play as Kerch Labs. In this policy, "we" means Kerch Labs. Questions about your data go to stellarfocus.support@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in a database on your phone:

  • Focus sessions: planned and actual length, start and end time, and whether you finished them
  • The objects in your galaxy: type, color, position, when you earned them and any name you give them
  • Your Stardust balance and the history of how you earned and spent it
  • Missions, achievements and your streak
  • App settings, and whether you have finished the introduction screens
  • Which mailbox letters you have opened

3. Your account

You need an account to use Stellar Focus. Accounts are stored by Supabase, the service we use for sign-in and for our database. Your account contains:

  • Your email address and username
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. Supabase stores only a hashed version of it.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, profile picture link and account ID that Google shares at sign-in
  • When you last changed your username. A change costs 100 Stardust and is possible once every 24 hours, so nobody can switch names back and forth to pass as someone else.
  • Whether your account was created before August 4, 2026. We work this out from the date you signed up, and use it only to give early accounts the Founder's World planet and the mailbox letters meant for them.

We use the information Google shares only to create and run your Stellar Focus account. We do not share it with anyone or use it for advertising.

When you create an account, the app asks for your date of birth to check that you are at least 13. The check happens on your phone. The date is not stored, and it is not sent to us.

4. Cloud backup and session checks

While you are signed in, the app copies this data to your account:

  • Your focus sessions: planned and actual length, start and end time, status, the Stardust earned and your streak at the time
  • The objects in your galaxy: type, color, position, when you earned them, the focus minutes behind them and any name you give them
  • Your Stardust history: the amount, reason and time of each entry

The backup lets you get your galaxy back when you sign in on a new phone.

When a session starts, the app sends its planned length and start time to our server. When it ends, the app asks the server to confirm it. We use these checks only to stop rewards for sessions that did not happen. If the server cannot be reached, you keep your reward.

If a session ends while your phone is offline, the app sends the same data the next time it is opened with a connection.

5. Friends

Friends are optional. Nothing in this section applies to you until you share your friend code or enter someone else's.

Your account gets a friend code the first time you open the Friends screen. Nobody can search for you by username or email. Someone can send you a friend request only if they have your code, and you become friends only after one of you accepts.

Once you are friends with someone, they can see:

  • Your username
  • Your galaxy: the type and color of each object, the day you earned it and the focus minutes behind it. This includes the dead stars left by sessions you ended early. Friends see the day only, never the time of day.
  • On the weekly leaderboard: how many minutes you focused since Monday (00:00 UTC) and in how many sessions. Only sessions our server could confirm are counted.
  • Only if you turn on "Show friends when I am focusing": a "focusing now" note next to your name while a session of yours is running.

Your friends cannot see:

  • Your email address or the details Google shares at sign-in
  • The names you give your sessions or your objects
  • Your Stardust balance or its history

A person who has sent you a request that you have not answered sees only that the request is open. They see your username once you accept. When you send a request, the person who receives it sees your username.

We store your friend code, and for each friendship or open request the two accounts, who asked, whether it was accepted, and when. You can remove a friend or withdraw a request at any time in the Friends screen. The other person then loses access to your galaxy straight away.

You can also block someone. Blocking ends any friendship or open request between you, and their later requests no longer reach you. They are not told. We store who blocked whom and when, until you unblock them.

To stop friend codes from being guessed, we record the time of each friend request you send, including requests with a wrong code, and allow 20 in 24 hours. Nobody can see these records. Records older than two days are deleted the next time you send a request, and all of them when you delete your account.

6. The global board and your sharing choices

When you set up the app, it asks what you want to share. Every choice starts switched off, and you can change them at any time under Me → Privacy & sharing. We store your choices and when you answered, so the app does not ask again on another phone.

  • Appear on the global board. Everyone signed in to Stellar Focus can see the weekly board: your username and how many minutes you focused since Monday (00:00 UTC). The same counting rules as the friends leaderboard apply. The board also names last week's best.
  • Hide my name there. You appear on the board as "Explorer" instead of your username.
  • Let people on the board visit my galaxy. People who see you on the board can open your galaxy and see the same things a friend sees there (section 5), and nothing else.
  • Show friends when I am focusing. Accepted friends see a "focusing now" note while a session of yours is running. People who are not your friends never see it.

When you switch a choice off, the change applies straight away.

7. Mailbox

The mailbox shows letters from us, for example news about the app or a gift for early accounts. Letters come with the app or from our server. Only we can write them. Which letters you have opened is stored on your phone, and the app does not tell us whether you read them.

8. What we do not collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos or files
  • Which apps are installed on your phone
  • Your advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • Analytics, advertising or crash-reporting data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

The current version does not block other apps. If we add app blocking, we will update this policy before that version is released.

When you share your galaxy, the app makes a picture of it on your phone and hands it to the app you pick in Android's share menu. The picture contains no username, email or balance, and it is not sent to us.

9. Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and backup on servers in Stockholm, Sweden, and processes this data on our behalf.
  • Account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset links, are sent through Gmail from our support address. Google processes your email address and the message to deliver them.
  • Google handles the sign-in step if you choose Sign in with Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.
  • Google Play distributes the app. Google Play and Android may collect diagnostic data under their own settings and policies.
  • If the app asks you to rate it, the rating window belongs to Google Play. Your rating and review go to Google, and the app does not learn whether you rated it or what you wrote.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the account pages linked from it. GitHub may log your IP address when you open them.

Apart from what your friends can see (section 5) and what you choose to show on the global board (section 6), we do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

10. Why we use your data

We use your account and backup data to provide the app you signed up for, including restoring your galaxy on a new phone and, if you add friends, showing them the things listed in section 5 (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We show you on the global board, let people on it visit your galaxy and show friends when you are focusing only because you switched these on, and you can switch them off at any time (consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). We use session checks and the friend request limit to keep rewards and the boards fair and friend codes safe, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

11. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Your account, backup and session checks stay until you delete your account. They are all deleted together.
  • A friendship or friend request stays until one of you removes it or deletes their account.
  • A block stays until you unblock the person or one of you deletes their account.
  • Records of friend requests you sent are deleted as described in section 5, at the latest when you delete your account.
  • Your sharing choices stay until you change them or delete your account.
  • If you email us, we keep the email only as long as we need it to answer you.

12. Deleting your data

To delete the data on your phone, use Me → Reset all data in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This does not delete your cloud backup.

To delete your account and everything stored with it, including friendships, blocks and your sharing choices, use Me → Request account deletion in the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page, or email us. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

13. Children

Stellar Focus is not meant for children under 13, and the app does not let them create an account. If you think a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data travels between the app and Supabase over an encrypted connection. Database rules let each account read and write only its own data. There are two exceptions. An accepted friend can read the items listed in section 5, and people who are signed in can read what you chose to show on the global board (section 6). Both happen only through server functions that check the friendship or your choice first and return nothing else. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and device encryption, and the app does not add its own encryption on top. The app's data is left out of Android's automatic device backups.

15. Your rights

If you live in the EU or the UK, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, and ask for it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We answer within 30 days.

16. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what data leaves your phone, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

17. Contact

Email: stellarfocus.support@gmail.com. We use what you send us only to answer you, and we aim to reply within 5 business days.
