Privacy Policy

Stellar Focus by Kerch Labs · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Stellar Focus keeps your sessions and your galaxy on your phone. Because the app needs an account, it also keeps a backup of that data in your account, so you can restore it on a new phone. We do not sell your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

1. Who we are

Stellar Focus is an Android app made by Kerem Mert, who publishes it on Google Play as Kerch Labs. In this policy, "we" means Kerch Labs. Questions about your data go to stellarfocus.support@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your phone

The app keeps the following in a database on your phone:

  • Focus sessions: planned and actual length, start and end time, and whether you finished them
  • The objects in your galaxy: type, color, name, position and when you earned them
  • Your Stardust balance and the history of how you earned and spent it
  • Missions, achievements and your streak
  • App settings, and whether you have finished the introduction screens

3. Your account

You need an account to use Stellar Focus. Accounts are stored by Supabase, the service we use for sign-in and for our database. Your account contains:

  • Your email address and username
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. Supabase stores only a hashed version of it.
  • If you sign in with Google: the name, profile picture link and account ID that Google shares at sign-in

We use the information Google shares only to create and run your Stellar Focus account. We do not share it with anyone or use it for advertising.

When you create an account, the app asks for your date of birth to check that you are at least 13. The check happens on your phone. The date is not stored, and it is not sent to us.

4. Cloud backup and session checks

While you are signed in, the app copies this data to your account:

  • Your focus sessions: planned and actual length, start and end time, status, the Stardust earned and your streak at the time
  • The objects in your galaxy: type, color, name, position, when you earned them and the focus minutes behind them
  • Your Stardust history: the amount, reason and time of each entry

The backup lets you get your galaxy back when you sign in on a new phone.

When a session starts, the app sends its planned length and start time to our server. When it ends, the app asks the server to confirm it. We use these checks only to stop rewards for sessions that did not happen. If the server cannot be reached, you keep your reward.

5. What we do not collect

  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos or files
  • Which apps are installed on your phone
  • Your advertising ID or other device identifiers
  • Analytics, advertising or crash-reporting data. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

The current version does not block other apps. If we add app blocking, we will update this policy before that version is released.

6. Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and backup on servers in Stockholm, Sweden, and processes this data on our behalf.
  • Account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset links, are sent through Gmail from our support address. Google processes your email address and the message to deliver them.
  • Google handles the sign-in step if you choose Sign in with Google. Google's own privacy policy applies to that step.
  • Google Play distributes the app. Google Play and Android may collect diagnostic data under their own settings and policies.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the account pages linked from it. GitHub may log your IP address when you open them.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

7. Why we use your data

We use your account and backup data to provide the app you signed up for, including restoring your galaxy on a new phone (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We use session checks to keep rewards fair, which is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

8. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Your account, backup and session checks stay until you delete your account. They are all deleted together.
  • If you email us, we keep the email only as long as we need it to answer you.

9. Deleting your data

To delete the data on your phone, use Settings → Reset All Data in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. This does not delete your cloud backup.

To delete your account and everything stored with it, use Settings → Request Account Deletion in the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page, or email us. We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

10. Children

Stellar Focus is not meant for children under 13, and the app does not let them create an account. If you think a child under 13 has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data travels between the app and Supabase over an encrypted connection. Database rules let each account read and write only its own data. Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox and device encryption, and the app does not add its own encryption on top.

12. Your rights

If you live in the EU or the UK, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, and ask for it in a portable format. You can also complain to a data protection authority. To use any of these rights, email us. We answer within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects what data leaves your phone, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

14. Contact

Email: stellarfocus.support@gmail.com. We use what you send us only to answer you, and we aim to reply within 5 business days.
